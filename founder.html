About the Founder

A vision centered on genuine relationships.

Olajide Segun Hammed founded Bastos Matchmaking with a focus on creating opportunities for people who are serious about finding genuine relationships.

His work through Bastos Matchmaking is centered on intentional dating, meaningful introductions, respectful communication, and creating an environment where people can explore relationships at their own pace.

The platform is designed to connect relationship-minded adults while providing tools that allow members to create profiles, discover other members, and begin conversations.

The Vision

Making meaningful connection more intentional.

The vision behind Bastos Matchmaking is to provide a welcoming digital environment where people can approach dating with greater purpose.
