About the Organization

Bastos Matchmaking

Bastos Matchmaking is a matchmaking platform focused on helping adults discover meaningful connections and approach relationships with intention.

The platform provides an online environment where relationship-minded adults can create profiles, discover potential connections, and communicate while exploring compatibility.

Bastos Matchmaking is founded by Olajide Segun Hammed.
