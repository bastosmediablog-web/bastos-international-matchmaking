About Bastos Matchmaking

Meaningful connections begin with intention.

Bastos Matchmaking is a relationship-focused matchmaking platform created for people who are serious about finding genuine connections and building meaningful relationships.

Our Mission

Bastos Matchmaking was created around a simple idea: meeting the right person should be about more than appearances or casual interactions.

Our goal is to provide an environment where people can introduce themselves honestly, discover compatible people, and build relationships based on shared values, interests, communication, and long-term intentions.

Who We Serve

Bastos Matchmaking is designed for adults who are interested in intentional dating and meaningful relationships.

Members can create profiles, discover other members, communicate, and explore potential relationships at their own pace.

Our Approach

Founder

Bastos Matchmaking was founded by Olajide Segun Hammed, who is also known publicly as Olajide Bastos.

The founder's vision is to create a professional matchmaking environment where people seeking genuine relationships can meet and communicate in a thoughtful setting.
